Picture size
Higher is sharper but needs more from the phone and makes bigger files. 720p is a good start.
Orientation
Portrait is for vertical video (stories, phone screens).
Frame rate
60 (the default) gives the smoothest motion and fades, but needs more from the phone, takes more memory and makes larger files. If the phone struggles or the page reloads, choose 30 or 24.

Smarter matching (AI)
An AI model (CLIP) looks at what is in your loops and groups similar ones, which gives smoother sets. It downloads about 90 MB the first time (about 150 MB with a theme), so use Wi-Fi. It takes a little longer before the video starts. It is heavy for the phone: if the page reloads on its own, the phone ran out of memory, so switch it off or use fewer loops. If the AI cannot start, the app carries on without it.
Loops that match the theme are preferred. Needs smarter matching switched on.
