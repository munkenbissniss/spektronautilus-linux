Live Video DJ Mixer

Mix complete music videos like a DJ. Built to be solid at events, with an output made for an HDMI projector.

Open the mixerPhone version

The full mixer is made for a computer with Chrome or Edge. On a phone you are sent to a simpler version with two decks, crossfader, effects, posters, full screen and a way to send the picture to another screen. It is new: test it at home before a show, and keep a backup plan.

What it does

  • Two decks for music videos (mp4, mov, webm). Sound and picture mix together.
  • Crossfader by hand, or one button for an automatic fade. Auto-mix can even line up the next track for you.
  • Preview of both decks next to the output.
  • Your own logo in the upper right corner.
  • Sliding text posters in preset colours, saved in slots. They can show the track name by themselves.
  • MIDI control with learn.
  • A clean output window for the projector, and a recorder.

Built to not crash

Tracks are loaded fully into memory before they play. A problem with one file shows a message and never stops the other deck. The picture keeps running even when the control window is in the background, and closing the window by accident asks first.

Streaming and casting

Capture the output window in OBS (Window Capture) to stream it, or use Chrome's Cast option and pick the output window. The mixer itself does not send video over the internet.
